homelab

homelab is a personal, non-commercial home server project by Anton Lysenko.

One of its components is a private nutrition and activity tracker. It reads the owner's own activity data (calories burned, steps, workouts, sleep and heart-rate metrics) from the Google Health API, once the owner signs in and grants read-only access, and shows it in a private Telegram bot and dashboard on the home server.

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.
